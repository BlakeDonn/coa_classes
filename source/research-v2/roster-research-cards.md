# CoA specialization research cards

Generated from the August 5 structural snapshot and the current evidence packets. These are research cards, not a tier list. A reported DPS value is reproduced as a limited source signal and is not a recommendation.

## Barbarian

### Brutality

- **Role and range:** Damage; Melee.
- **Structural baseline:** A heavy-weapon brawler built around large swings, Whirls, Storm of Steel, and execute attacks such as Decapitate. It wants to build momentum and Fury, then cash out below 35% health; it has strong cleave and self-sustain, but its best damage requires melee uptime and correct execute timing.
- **Current evidence themes:** cross-spec damage impression, leveling comparison, leveling sustain, permanent companion, pet ability unlocks, weapon scaling.
- **Community documentation:** 2 indexed Build Hub build(s); reported DPS 4056 (damage); 120 Mythic upload record(s).
- **Evidence IDs:** reddit:barbarian-ancestry-2026-07-13, reddit:barbarian-brutality-2026-07-08.
- **Curation:** validated overlay.

### Headhunting

- **Role and range:** Damage; Ranged.
- **Structural baseline:** A fully mobile axe, spear, and javelin thrower. It stacks armor reduction and bleeds, builds Born in Blood and Enrage synergies, and uses rapid throws for single-target pressure; recent players describe strong single target and modest cleave, with weak true AoE.
- **Current evidence themes:** cross-spec damage impression, leveling comparison, leveling sustain, permanent companion, pet ability unlocks, weapon scaling.
- **Community documentation:** 3 indexed Build Hub build(s); reported DPS 4303 (damage); 15 Mythic upload record(s).
- **Evidence IDs:** reddit:barbarian-ancestry-2026-07-13, reddit:barbarian-brutality-2026-07-08.
- **Curation:** validated overlay.

### Ancestry

- **Role and range:** Support; Melee.
- **Structural baseline:** A frost-and-ale warleader with a summoned Honored Ancestor, Tankard Fill Level, Keg Smash, and a large collection of raid buffs. War Cry, Tavern Brawl, Clanlord's Totem, Ramhorn Rage, and Warband make it one of the clearest offensive-support trees; the cost is many situational buttons and buff-window management.
- **Current evidence themes:** leveling comparison, permanent companion, pet ability unlocks.
- **Community documentation:** 1 indexed Build Hub build(s); reported DPS 2127 (support_or_offrole); 18 Mythic upload record(s).
- **Evidence IDs:** reddit:barbarian-ancestry-2026-07-13.
- **Curation:** validated overlay.

## Witch Doctor

### Voodoo

- **Role and range:** Damage; Ranged.
- **Structural baseline:** A Shadow-heavy damage-over-time and delayed-burst caster. Puppeteer's Threads stores part of the damage you deal, while Bad Juju, Hexfire, Shadow Puppets, wards, golems, and spirit cooldowns build toward large detonation windows. Strong multi-target potential comes with setup, DoT tracking, and cooldown alignment.
- **Current evidence themes:** AoE and mana frustration, Mimic Ward transition, clone window, kiting, leveling friction, single-target sequence, slows.
- **Community documentation:** 1 indexed Build Hub build(s); reported DPS 5419 (damage); 62 Mythic upload record(s).
- **Evidence IDs:** reddit:witch-doctor-2026-07-31, reddit:witch-doctor-voodoo-2026-07-29.
- **Curation:** validated overlay.

### Brewing

- **Role and range:** Healer, Support; Hybrid.
- **Structural baseline:** A modular alchemist healer. Ingredients, Bases, and Mojos change the effects of the Cauldron and Potion Toss, so the player prepares the right brew for steady healing, shields, raid healing, or damage support. It is flexible and utility-rich, but less plug-and-play than a conventional direct healer.
- **Current evidence themes:** button-count disagreement, level-dependent complexity, new-player fit.
- **Community documentation:** 5 indexed Build Hub build(s); no DPS value in the source snapshot; 126 Mythic upload record(s).
- **Evidence IDs:** reddit:easy-specs-roster-2026-08-03.
- **Curation:** validated overlay.

### Shadowhunting

- **Role and range:** Damage; Ranged.
- **Structural baseline:** A mobile bow-and-voodoo hybrid that collects Spirits through Reclamation and Malefic Arrow, then spends them on Spirit Eclipse, glaives, wards, or Frenzied Spirits. It combines sustained ranged damage, self-healing, summons, and burst, but asks the player to juggle two damage scalings and a Spirit cycle.
- **Current evidence themes:** AoE and mana frustration, Mimic Ward transition, class comparison, clone window, documentation gap, kiting, leveling friction, single-target sequence, slows.

- **Structural baseline:** A fast, crit-driven glaive fighter. It generates Felfury, spends it on Azzinoth's Assault, uses charges and Chaos Rush to stay active, and turns critical strikes into off-hand cleave. Recent builds emphasize very high AoE potential and good mobility.

### Infernal

- **Role and range:** Damage; Ranged.
- **Structural baseline:** A Shadowflame caster built around Fel Fireball, Felstrikes, Ruin, critical strikes, and Inner Demon. Crits feed more damage-over-time effects and resource refunds, so the tree scales sharply when its feedback loop is running; missed procs or poor Felfury control make it feel much weaker.

### Tyrant

- **Role and range:** Tank; Hybrid.
- **Structural baseline:** A dual-two-handed demonic tank that converts avoidance into armor and magic resistance, uses Carve and retaliation for threat, and leeches or heals through aggression. Its defining cooldowns include Tyrannical Resolve and group mitigation through Infernal Whipcrack. Recent players describe extremely high durability, though that does not prove comparative balance.

## Stormbringer

### Lightning

- **Role and range:** Damage; Ranged.
- **Structural baseline:** A direct-damage lightning caster that builds Static, marks targets with Volt, and spends on Arm of Thorim, Call Lightning, and Electrocute. Standing still builds Electrical Charge toward a no-depletion window, so it rewards planned turret phases while retaining a lightning dash for repositioning.
- **Current evidence themes:** BG versus duel split, Static builder-spender, Thorim burst, channeling, hard-cast setup, mana recovery, new-player comparison, retail Elemental comparison, single-target versus AoE, support context, support role.

### Wind

- **Role and range:** Support; Ranged.
- **Structural baseline:** An Air Elemental controller with Updrafts, movement tools, pulls, rescue gates, and party buffs. Tempest's Call and Surge of Might provide major offensive windows, while Wind Gate, Evacuate, Unshackle, and Raging Zephyr control positioning. Its personal damage exists, but current builds most often frame it as support.

Thunder Orbs, Power Spheres, elementals, Drown, and Stormcloud add layered target setup. It has burst and battlefield activity, but more object and stack tracking than Lightning.
