Restyle Mockups v3

One family section (Combo Sequencers, real data) plus the Artificer profile in each. All three: compact header replacing the bulky hero, redesigned toggles, more visual cards, and a TL;DR versus detail split. All three are phone-friendly. Pick one; the pick becomes the production restyle spec.

A · Ascension Chrome

Faithful lift of ascension.gg's language.

Look
Black chrome nav, gold serif display, gold outline and fill buttons, underline segmented toggles, one-line masthead.
Profile
TL;DR and Full-codex toggle. TL;DR is fantasy plus loop plus six gold complexity bars plus one enjoy and one skip verdict.
Tradeoff
Safest and most on-brand, least distinctive. Closest to the current site's bones.

B · War Plate

In-game UI language: plates, pips, item tooltips.

Look
Braided double-border plates with corner gems, spec icons, stone segmented toggles, item-quality colors for research state (gold curated, grey in progress).
Profile
An item-tooltip IS the TL;DR: quality-colored name, pip stats, flavor line, verdicts. "Read the full codex" reveals detail.
Tradeoff
Most game-flavored and most visual. Heaviest chrome; risks kitsch if overdone.

C · Editorial Atlas

Magazine treatment; typography does the work.

Look
Numbered family spreads, hairline gold rules, roster rows instead of cards, a six-cell complexity heat matrix per spec.
Profile
A dossier: italic lede, six radial gauges, accordion folds for detail. TL;DR is everything above the folds.
Tradeoff
Most elegant and scannable at 70 specs. Least "game UI"; rows are less swatchy than cards.
Shared ground rules: real data from the production explorer-data.js. Qualitative complexity, honest unresearched states, no fun scores. Serif is a Cinzel-style stand-in for ascension.gg's Beaufort; production would bundle Cinzel (open license). Elements mix freely — pick a base and name the swaps.
