Concept A · Atlas first, tray second

Find specs by how they feel.

Six feel-families group specs by temperament, not by class or role. Read the family that sounds like you, open a spec for the full profile, and add up to three to the tray to compare.

Prototype coverage: 12 of 70 specs are deeply profiled here. In production every spec joins exactly one primary family, with a "also fits" cross-link when it straddles two. Family membership is an editorial inference and is labeled as such.

About this concept

Decision flow

Recognize yourself in a family → browse that shelf → open spec profiles → shortlist up to three in the tray → compare side by side → follow the evidence links out.

What it does best

Cold-start discovery by temperament. A player who knows "I like planning, I hate being punished" lands in the right neighborhood in one scroll, without knowing any CoA vocabulary.

Main tradeoff

The families are themselves our inference. Specs that straddle groups (Farstrider is both commander and strategist) get flattened into one shelf, and comparison is a second step rather than the default view.
