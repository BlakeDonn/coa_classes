Concept B · Comparison is the home page

Put three specs on the bench.

The workbench is always visible. Pick candidates from the roster, and every trait, complexity facet, and evidence claim lines up row by row. Turn on "differences only" to hide everything the candidates share.

12 of 70 specs profiled in this prototype.

Rows where all benched specs match are dimmed (or hidden).

About this concept

Decision flow

Filter or search the roster → click to bench up to three specs → scan aligned rows and hide the ties → eliminate one at a time → follow build links for the survivor.

What it does best

Head-to-head elimination. When you already have candidates ("Infinite or Sentinel?"), the aligned rows and the differences-only toggle answer it fastest of the three concepts.

Main tradeoff

Weakest cold start. An undecided player faces an empty bench and a flat roster with no narrative on-ramp, and the dense grid is the closest of the three to a spreadsheet.

Still needs research
