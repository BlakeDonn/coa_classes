Class exploration redesign · 2026-08-05

Three ways to pick a main.

All three concepts blend a playstyle atlas with a comparison tray, using real August 5 project data for 12 deeply profiled specs. They differ in which step leads the experience. Open each, play with it, pick a direction.

Concept A

Playstyle Atlas

Six feel-families as browsable shelves. The tray docks at the bottom; comparison is a second step.

Decision flow
Recognize your temperament → browse the shelf → open profiles → tray → compare.
Best at
Cold-start discovery by feel, with zero CoA vocabulary required.
Main tradeoff
Family membership is editorial inference, and straddler specs get flattened into one shelf.
Needs research
Validating family assignments and choosing families for all 58 unprofiled specs.

Concept B

Decision Workbench

The three-slot comparison IS the home page. A roster rail feeds it; a differences-only toggle hides ties.

Decision flow
Filter the roster → bench up to three → hide the ties → eliminate → follow evidence links.
Best at
Head-to-head elimination when you already hold candidates.
Main tradeoff
Weak cold start and the highest spreadsheet risk of the three.
Needs research
Whether 1-5 positions are defensible side by side, and which rows drive real eliminations.

Concept C

Fit Interview

Five plain questions sort every spec into reasoned buckets. Every match shows its working; nothing is scored.

Decision flow
Answer questions → read reasoned buckets → open profiles → pin → compare pinned.
Best at
Turning self-knowledge directly into candidates for a brand-new player.
Main tradeoff
Matching rules are inference on inference; bad thresholds silently misroute people.
Needs research
Whether the five questions predict enjoyment, plus threshold tuning and reason-reading tests.

Ground rules all three share

  • No universal fun score. Traits are descriptive positions; buckets and families are labeled inference.
  • Complexity is five dimensions — buttons, tracking, setup, reactive calls, mistake punishment — never one number.
  • Every value carries an evidence tier: snapshot data, player reports, or our inference.
  • Leveling shift is a first-class field. Infinite, Sentinel, and Mechanics all change or conflict at higher levels, and the UI says so.
  • Videos are honest. The August 5 snapshot verified zero videos, so every video slot shows the empty state and the schema a verified video would need.
  • Real data only. Build counts, titles, links, and player-report claims come from the August 5 research snapshot. The 12 profiles cover your featured specs plus Brigand, Animation, Ancestry, and Archery for family coverage.
